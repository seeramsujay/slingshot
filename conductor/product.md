# DustVision: Adaptive Dust Mitigation System

## Overview
DustVision is a smart-city framework designed to detect, forecast, and mitigate construction-driven dust pollution (PM2.5 & PM10). It leverages multi-sensor data fusion and machine learning to provide proactive air quality management.

## Core Features
1. **Sensor Simulation**: Generates realistic time-series pollution data with spikes.
2. **Camera-Event Simulation**: Synthetic computer vision triggers for visible dust events.
3. **Fusion Engine**: Multi-modal confirmation of dust events using sensors, cameras, and wind data.
4. **ML Forecasting**: Short-term predictive modeling of PM levels.
5. **Mitigation Engine**: Rule-based deployment of smart sprinklers and compliance enforcement.

## Target Users
- **Smart City Operators**: For ward-level air quality management.
- **Construction Contractors**: For compliance and automated mitigation.
- **Environmental Agencies**: For urban monitoring and governance.

## Success Metrics
- **Forecasting Accuracy**: MAE/RMSE of PM predictions.
- **Mitigation Effectiveness**: Reduction in peak PM levels after intervention.
- **Compliance Score**: Tracking contractor adherence to mitigation protocols.
